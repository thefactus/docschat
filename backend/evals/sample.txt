Employee Leave and Remote Work Policy

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused leave days to the next calendar year.
Any unused annual leave above that carryover limit expires at year end unless
approved by People Operations.

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually.

Full-time employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to
manager approval and team coverage needs.
